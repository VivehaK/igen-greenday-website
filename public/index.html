01 / WHY WE ACT

Awareness is the beginning.
Action is the impact.

IGEN GREENDAY aims to establish 22 October as World Green Action Implementation Day, inspiring citizens and organisations to turn sustainability commitments into measurable green actions.

Rooted in the Panchabhutas and aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the movement brings communities together for a greener, healthier and more resilient Mother Earth.
